Data-Sparse Science

Statistical Theory for the Microscopic Ocean

What does the ocean look like on scales of 1 micron to 1 centimeter? Here, I am developing a quantitative theory to account for the effect of microorganisms swimming and dieing in generating sustained microscopic fluctuations.

Chiral Particles

How does a particle's shape determine the pattern it traces as it falls? Here we show that particles which differ from their mirror image (like a propeller or a spiral) can trace complex trajectories due to their broken symmetry.

Data-Rich Science

Advection Heads in an Atmosphere Foundation Model

Advection describes the transport of information by a velocity field, and is a fundamental process in flow physics. We study this process in Aurora, a foundation model for atmospheric dynamics, and discover "advection heads" which appear to be responsible for implementing this physical process.
